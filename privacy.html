Privacy

This website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind, and it does not ask for or store personal data.

The only thing saved is your language preference, kept in your own browser (local storage) so the site opens in the language you chose. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

If you email us, we will use your address only to reply to you.

The social network links lead to third-party sites, which have their own privacy policies.
